## Refrigeration fundamentals

- **[How Copeland Scroll Digital Works](https://inspectapedia.com/aircond/How-Copeland-Scroll-Digital-Works-Emerson.pdf)** - Emerson / Copeland (hosted by InspectAPedia). Explains digital scroll capacity modulation: solenoid energized = unloaded (scrolls separate axially, no pumping); de-energized = loaded (full capacity); average capacity set by the loaded/unloaded time ratio. _Equipment: Copeland Digital Scroll compressors._ (id: `copeland-digital`)
- **[Copeland Digital Compressor Controller - Application Engineering Bulletin](https://webapps2.copeland.com/ci/digital-compressor-controller/ae-bulletin.pdf)** - Copeland. Manufacturer bulletin for the controller that sits between a digital compressor and the system controller and drives the unloading solenoid. Useful for understanding digital compressor control and protection; details are model-specific. _Equipment: Copeland Scroll Digital and Discus Digital compressors._ (id: `copeland-dcc`)
- **[Axial, Radial and Digital Scrolls](https://hvacrschool.com/axial-radial-and-digital-scrolls/)** - HVAC School. Technician-friendly explanation of axial/radial compliance and digital scroll loading/unloading, including the unusual sound and cycling amp draw of a digital scroll. _Equipment: Scroll compressors (general)._ (id: `hvacr-digital-scroll`)
- **[Superheat and Subcooling Basics (podcast/article)](https://hvacrschool.com/podcasts/superheat-and-subcooling-basics-2)** - HVAC School. Recap of superheat, subcooling, sensible vs latent heat, metering devices and target vs measured values. Notes that for zeotropic blends, superheat uses dew point and subcooling uses bubble point. _Equipment: General._ (id: `hvacr-superheat-basics`)
- **[AC Pressures, Subcooling and Superheat (video)](https://hvacrschool.com/videos/ac-pressures-subcooling-and-superheat/)** - HVAC School. Video explaining how suction/discharge pressure, superheat and subcooling relate, including subcooling as an indicator of liquid 'stacked' in the condenser. _Equipment: General._ (id: `hvacr-pressures`)
- **[Solving Superheat](https://hvacrschool.com/solving-superheat/)** - HVAC School. How to interpret superheat readings as diagnostic data. _Equipment: General._ (id: `hvacr-solving-superheat`)
- **[Section 608 Technician Certification](https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-technician-certification-0)** - U.S. EPA. Official EPA page on 608 certification (Core, Type I, II, III, Universal). Technicians who open refrigerant circuits must be certified. _Equipment: All stationary refrigeration and A/C._ (id: `epa-608`)
- **[Stationary Refrigeration and Air Conditioning (Section 608 overview)](https://www.epa.gov/section608)** - U.S. EPA. EPA rules on refrigerant handling, venting prohibition, leak repair and recovery. _Equipment: All stationary refrigeration and A/C._ (id: `epa-608-overview`)
- **[The Professor: Digital Capacity Control for Compressors](https://www.achrnews.com/articles/108947-the-professor-digital-capacity-control-for-compressors)** - ACHR News. Trade-magazine explanation of digital (pulse-width) capacity control. _Equipment: Digital scroll compressors._ (id: `achr-digital-capacity`)
